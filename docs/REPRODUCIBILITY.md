# Reproducing the STACI calculations

## Required software

Use MATLAB R2026a with Optimization Toolbox and Global Optimization Toolbox, the MATLAB MEX interface from the [STACI repository](https://github.com/hoscsaba/staci), and EPANET 2.2 with the EPANET-MATLAB Toolkit for independent verification. Build the current STACI checkout: its TT Pareto numerical fallback requires `setSolverTolerances`, which older MEX binaries do not expose. The optimizer keeps one `StaciModel` in memory, updates pipe diameters there, and calls the hydraulic and direct stationary-quality solvers. It writes no per-candidate INP or quality history.

## Tanyimboh–Templeman: full calculation sequence

Run these stages from the repository root in MATLAB R2026a. Set `paper_root = pwd`, `staci_root` to the STACI checkout, and `mex_dir` to its compiled MATLAB MEX directory. The final TT outputs are in `networks/tanyimboh_templeman/results_staci_10_120_clsplit_v2/`. Superseded staging runs have been removed.

1. **Import and freeze the input.** Load `networks/tanyimboh_templeman/input/network_staci_fixed_load.inp`. It has eight variable physical pipes of 100 m each. Node 1 itself is the 50-m fixed-head source reservoir; node 2 has a prescribed negative demand as the second input. No intermediate source pipe exists, and only P1--P8 are diameter variables and failure candidates.
2. **Fix the model and feasible set.** Keep the input's demand, elevations, source heads, roughness, reaction rates and topology fixed. Set every design diameter to the continuous 0.1–64 in range (2.54–1625.6 mm). Enforce 10–120 m at assessment junctions and at least 0.2 mg/L stationary chlorine at consumer nodes whose demand exceeds the documented material-demand threshold. TT has no pressure anchors. The chlorine objective calculates *outgoing chlorine-mass splitting* using downstream pipe residuals and a consumer-withdrawal branch.
3. **Compute frozen topology weights.** `tanyimboh_branch_hop_weights` counts undirected branching hops from the two supply entries. This uses no hydraulic optimization. The weights are fixed throughout every branch-hop GA.
4. **Run the six unrestricted single-objective searches.** `run_uniform_nominal_optimizations(staci_root,mex_dir,'tanyimboh_templeman','results_staci_10_120_clsplit_v2')` varies all eight diameters for cost, hydraulic entropy, branch-hop entropy, squared chlorine concentration, chlorine-mass-splitting entropy and Todini. The initial cost GA stopped with an infeasible penalty result and retained a chlorine-design warm start. Run `rerun_tanyimboh_cost(staci_root,mex_dir)` from `networks/tanyimboh_templeman` to correct that endpoint: it screens 28 connected five-pipe supports with four log-diameter SQP starts each, reruns the GA from the best feasible seed, and checks the selected vector in EPANET before saving. Each candidate evaluation uses resident STACI and no per-candidate INP files. The local scan uses a 0.02 m inward pressure margin for numerical agreement; the final design satisfies the published 10–120 m and 0.2 mg/L constraints.
5. **Re-evaluate and reconcile the six endpoints.** `export_tanyimboh_stage_tables(paper_root,staci_root,mex_dir)` evaluates every objective on every saved diameter vector. The refined cost design and chlorine-concentration design are distinct: the latter costs more for a small improvement in its optimized chlorine score.
6. **Calculate pipe rankings, then run restricted GAs.** `run_staci_ranking_studies(staci_root,mex_dir,'tanyimboh_templeman',{'cost','hydraulic_entropy','branch_hop_weighted_entropy','chlorine_concentration','chlorine_entropy','todini'},result_dir,figure_dir)` first calculates five fixed pipe orders, then runs distinct nested searches with `K=0,...,8` free diameters for each objective and order. Every `(objective, ranking, K)` point is cached separately; extending `K` does not repeat earlier points. At `K=8`, check equality with the unrestricted saved endpoint to objective tolerance. This stage is logically separate from step 4. The Todini subset evaluator explicitly calls `wdn.staci_todini_index`; its cache version invalidates older subset records that lacked this fitness value. If a feasible subset exceeds the full-network score beyond numerical tolerance, use its diameter vector as a new full-search seed and repeat the endpoint check. With the source reservoir directly at node 1, Todini approaches 1 as the variable-pipe losses shrink; there is no fixed adapter loss.
7. **Sample and stress-test the cost–branch-hop trade-off.** `run_tanyimboh_pareto(paper_root,staci_root,mex_dir)` runs a separate `gamultiobj` search under the same constraints and saves its finite 96-point archive. `run_pareto_failure_replay(paper_root,staci_root,mex_dir,'tanyimboh_templeman')` then applies all eight PDA pipe failures to each point. Checkpoints are saved per archive index, so the replay can resume without repeating finished points. `manuscript/figures/make_pareto_failure_figures.m` plots worst water service as color and worst chlorine-compliant service as circle area. Its cost-sorted, numerically filtered TT envelope contains 94 of the 96 replayed points; the published S7 selects 20 existing points at approximately uniform arclength after normalizing cost and entropy, including both endpoints. It also retains the least-cost front point for which both worst-service indicators exceed 75% and the analogous point for 90%, marking their exact saved percentages with arrows. The complete archive and all failure-service results remain available. The archive is a finite sampled trade-off, not a proof of the continuous frontier.
8. **Replay eight pipe failures with PDA only.** `run_tanyimboh_failure_service(paper_root,staci_root,mex_dir)` closes each physical pipe in turn, retains the node-1 reservoir and node-2 injection, and holds saved diameters fixed. The requested-demand denominator is read from the fixed-load input without a DDA hydraulic solve. A pipe at the exact diameter lower bound is interpreted as removed when identifying the source-connected component; disconnected consumers receive zero delivery. Every connected hydraulic and chlorine state is first attempted by STACI with PDA; an EPANET PDA and extended-quality fallback resolves any remaining numerical nonconvergence. If node 2 is isolated from the fixed-head reservoir, its component is omitted and all consumers in it are counted as unserved.
9. **Verify, summarize, and render.** `validate_staci_final_designs` independently checks all six nominal vectors in EPANET 2.2, reporting maximum and mean absolute head, signed-flow and chlorine differences. `summarize_tanyimboh_vulnerability(paper_root)` computes the four main-text vulnerability indicators from the saved eight-case PDA CSV; it runs no solver. `export_tanyimboh_diameter_table(paper_root)` exports the six full-precision diameter columns in pipe-ID order in both millimeters and inches; `export_tanyimboh_stage_tables` also writes raw and best-relative nominal-objective CSVs. `make_figure3` redraws one 3-by-2 network matrix with signed m³/h pipe flows, actual pipe-flow arrows, horizontal source arrows entering nodes 1 and 2, and stationary-chlorine node colors. Source-arrow labels are obtained by summing the signed, strictly re-solved pipe flows incident on each source node, so they reflect each saved design rather than a hard-coded annotation. `make_tanyimboh_ranking_matrix(paper_root,draft_figures_dir)` draws the 2-by-6 cost/branch-hop ranking matrix; `make_tanyimboh_remaining_rankings(paper_root,draft_figures_dir)` draws the hydraulic-entropy/Todini and chlorine-concentration/chlorine-entropy 2-by-6 matrices from saved studies. `make_tanyimboh_submission_panels(paper_root,draft_figures_dir)` copies the network PNG/SVG into the Supplementary figures directory. The numerical CSVs, ranking caches, scenario-level PDA CSV, Pareto MAT, and validation CSV remain in the repository.

TT post hoc scoring uses a fresh STACI model for each saved diameter vector with RMS head-equation residual 1e-11 m and RMS mass residual 1e-12 kg/s. At the largest allowed diameters, head losses can be around 1e-6 m, below the importer's default 1e-4 m head residual limit; a resident solve may report convergence while retaining a prior trial's flow split. `export_tanyimboh_stage_tables`, the TT branch of `validate_staci_final_designs`, and `make_figure3` use the strict profile for nominal scoring and plotting. After `run_staci_ranking_studies`, call `revalue_tanyimboh_ranked_metrics(staci_root,mex_dir)` to rescore all 270 saved TT subset vectors on isolated models, update their cache and study metrics, and redraw the three TT ranking figures. It preserves all diameter vectors and the archived Todini optimized scores; no genetic search is repeated.

The analytical TT Table S4 maximizes entropy over flows subject only to continuity and nonnegative flow along the published arrows. No diameters, hydraulic head losses, or pressures enter that calculation. The hydraulic-entropy diameter search permits reversible EPANET pipes and enforces nominal pressure and chlorine bounds. Its saved P8 flow is −3.333 m³/h (from node 6 to node 5), giving entropy 2.43713 rather than the directed Case-A maximum 2.41098. The numerical source split is the same 20:35 in the two calculations; the admissible flow directions differ. Figure S3 uses signed m³/h labels relative to the reference arrows in Figure S2 and short arrows for actual solved directions.

To rebuild all three TT ranked-subset figures from the saved current MAT files, call `make_tanyimboh_ranking_matrix(paper_root,draft_figures_dir)` and `make_tanyimboh_remaining_rankings(paper_root,draft_figures_dir)` after adding `manuscript/figures` to the MATLAB path. The four added columns load `ranking_entropy_study.mat`, `ranking_todini_study.mat`, `ranking_chlorine_concentration_study.mat`, and `ranking_chlorine_entropy_study.mat` from `results_staci_10_120_clsplit_v2/`. Their top panels plot the optimized metric; each lower panel evaluates another metric on the same saved designs. This rendering step performs no GA or hydraulic simulation.

The Supplementary Material reports the analytical entropy benchmark, six-design cross-evaluation, and per-design nominal EPANET differences. The refined TT cost vector has 76.14% mean and 36.36% minimum water delivery in the eight saved pipe failures. A component containing only the fixed-flow node-2 injector has no pressure boundary, so it is excluded and its consumer demand is counted as unserved. This explicit rule is applied consistently to all six designs and Pareto points.

## Current Anytown sequence

The final nominal input is `networks/anytown/input/network_anchor_replay.inp`. R10 and pump P82 supply all 0.282644079872 m³/s of requested demand. R65/R165 are zero-flow pressure anchors: their terminal heads must remain within 215 ± 0.5 ft, but their fixed connectors carry no nominal water. The 38 physical pipes have 0.1–64 in continuous diameter bounds. All six objectives share 10–120 m assessment-junction pressure bounds and a stationary chlorine floor of 0.2 mg/L at material-demand consumers. Chlorine entropy uses outgoing chlorine-mass splitting, the same definition as the TT calculation. Branch-hop weights are calculated once from undirected topology with potential supply entries at the pump discharge and the two auxiliary reservoir connections.

1. To repeat the six unrestricted GAs from a clean result directory, run `run_uniform_nominal_optimizations(staci_root,mex_dir,'anytown','results_staci_anchor_replay')`. Save or remove the released endpoint MAT files first if a stochastic rerun is intended. The released MAT files and `ga_stop_six.csv` record the full-precision vectors and stopping data. Every candidate uses a resident STACI model; there is no per-candidate file I/O.
2. Run `summarize_anytown_physical_reservoir_results(paper_root,staci_root,mex_dir,'anchor_replay')` to cross-evaluate all six objectives on each final vector. This stage does not rerun a GA.
3. Run `run_anytown_anchor_replay_rankings(paper_root,staci_root,mex_dir,{'cost','hydraulic_entropy','branch_hop_weighted_entropy','chlorine_concentration','chlorine_entropy','todini'})`. Five pipe orders are frozen before subset optimization. Each objective and order releases K = 0,1,...,10,15,20,25,30,35,38 diameters. The K=0 original is an infeasible plotting reference; some K=1 searches are infeasible. Each subset result is cached independently, so a later extension need not repeat completed points. The first feasible incumbent is carried forward whenever a later search returns a worse objective value.
4. Run `verify_anytown_anchor_replay_rankings(paper_root,staci_root,mex_dir)`. It independently re-solves every feasible saved subset, checks the selected pipes and common constraints, verifies monotonic optimized trajectories after feasibility begins, and checks K=38 against the unrestricted endpoint. It writes `ranking_all_objectives_audit.csv`.
   Then run `refine_anytown_todini_rankings(paper_root,staci_root,mex_dir)` to reproduce Supplementary Figure S12. This runs constrained SQP from the saved Todini ranked-subset GA incumbents, carries the best feasible vector to larger nested subsets, and rechecks every selected vector on a fresh STACI model. It preserves the original GA archive, writes `ranking_todini_refined_study.mat` and CSV, and redraws the hydraulic-entropy/Todini matrix. Each subset has its own reusable checkpoint; the independently optimized 38-pipe endpoint is unchanged. Local refinement improves best-found scores but does not prove global optimality.
5. Run `run_anytown_anchor_replay_pareto(paper_root,staci_root,mex_dir)` for a finite cost–branch-hop Pareto sample under the same nominal constraints. This is a separate multiobjective GA, not a reuse of a ranked-pipe trajectory. Next call `run_pareto_failure_replay(paper_root,staci_root,mex_dir,'anytown')` to replay the 114 PDA failure and reservoir-availability cases for each of the 275 feasible plotted points. Each point is checkpointed separately. `make_pareto_failure_figures` reads the completed service CSV and renders both networks' Supplementary plots without another hydraulic run.
6. Run `run_anytown_anchor_replay_outages(paper_root,staci_root,mex_dir)` and `summarize_anytown_anchor_replay_vulnerability(paper_root)`. Each saved diameter vector is transferred without rounding to `network_physical_reservoirs.inp`. R65/R165 regain their original 215-ft fixed heads and may supply or absorb water. Close each of the 38 physical pipes once under both reservoirs available, only R65 available, and only R165 available. R10/pump remains active. These 114 equally weighted cases per design use PDA hydraulics and stationary chlorine; no DDA solve is used in the outage loop. An unavailable reservoir is isolated by closing only its fixed connector. Worst 10% is the mean of the 12 largest service deficits.
7. Run `validate_reported_nominal_designs(staci_root,mex_dir)` as a required post-search EPANET 2.2 check of every displayed nominal vector before releasing figures or tables. It writes the resumable audit to `supplementary/results/nominal_design_validation/`. `validate_staci_final_designs` remains available for the detailed six-final-design Supplementary comparisons. Then run `reproduce_all_figures(staci_root,mex_dir)` and `export_manuscript_tables`.

The main-text Anytown design matrix and cost/branch-hop ranked-subset matrix read the original final MAT studies. The Supplementary hydraulic-entropy/Todini matrix reads the saved refined Todini study; the chlorine-objective matrix reads its original studies. Rendering performs no GA. The nominal design and failed-state models differ only in the documented reservoir availability and PDA conversion; the saved physical-pipe diameters are unchanged.

## Independent check and interpretation

`supplementary/validate_staci_final_designs.m` applies each final full-precision diameter vector to the matched nominal INP in EPANET 2.2. The current six-design Anytown CSV is `supplementary/results/validation_anytown_10_120_clsplit_final/staci_epanet_anytown_anchor_replay_six.csv`; it gives maximum and mean absolute differences in total head, signed link flow and chlorine. EPANET quality is advanced for 14 days with five-minute steps to approximate the stationary field. The saved vectors satisfy EPANET pressure, anchor-head, and material-consumer chlorine limits. The cost design has a 0.41083 mg/L all-node chlorine difference at stagnant zero-demand node 55; the supplementary PDF reports this exception alongside maximum and mean differences, STACI equations, and numerical tolerances.

The current Tanyimboh–Templeman calculation uses `results_staci_10_120_clsplit_v2/`, as described above. Balerma uses `results_staci_r38_anchor_r44_physical_0p1_64in_v1/`: R38 is a zero-flow 117 m nominal pressure anchor, while R43/R44/R88 remain physical supplies. In its stress test, all four reservoirs are restored; then a second set of cases makes R38 unavailable. Six direct reservoir connectors are protected and the remaining 448 pipes fail one at a time. The full-network six-objective, top-90 ranked-subset, and top-90 cost–branch-hop Pareto sequences are documented in [R38_ANCHOR_RUN.md](../networks/balerma/R38_ANCHOR_RUN.md). Bridge-pipe failures may use a temporary source-connected outage INP and count disconnected consumers as unserved. File handling occurs only in replay and verification, never in fitness evaluations.

The reported GA vectors are finite, best-found designs, not mathematical proofs of global optimality. Stress-test service values are conditional on the enumerated failures and one fixed demand state, not annual probabilities. Consult [the methods](METHODS.md) for exact boundaries, source handling, and service definitions.

## Released-data audit and full regeneration

Use the final result directories listed in the repository README. Exact full-precision diameters matter near active constraints. A fresh search should use a separate output directory, then pass all six designs through the same cross-evaluation, PDA replay, EPANET verification, and figure generation sequence before replacing released data. Stochastic GA endpoints are best found within the documented search budget, not global-optimality certificates.

For the published independent checks, call `validate_staci_final_designs` with the matching nominal INP and exact MAT vectors. The six final Anytown validation records are in `supplementary/results/validation_anytown_10_120_clsplit_final/`; TT uses `validation_tt_10_120_clsplit_v2/`, and Balerma uses its final result directory's `epanet_validation/`. EPANET quality is advanced for 14 days with five-minute quality steps to approximate the stationary STACI solution. All three designs apply 0.1–64 in diameter, 10–120 m assessment-junction pressure, and 0.2 mg/L material-consumer chlorine constraints to every objective. Anytown additionally enforces its two 215 ± 0.5 ft pressure anchors; Balerma enforces the R38 head anchor.

The current 1,369-row nominal audit includes the six final designs per
network, every diameter vector plotted in the ranked-subset figures, both
full TT and Anytown Pareto archives, and the 20 selected Balerma points. Run
`validate_reported_nominal_designs(staci_root,mex_dir)` after changing a
saved nominal MAT file and before releasing its figure or table. The audit
builds fresh matched STACI and EPANET models for each distinct vector, saves
resumable results under `supplementary/results/nominal_design_validation/`,
and rejects a resumed checkpoint if the source manifest changed. The
[audit guide](../supplementary/results/nominal_design_validation/README.md)
explains the agreement thresholds, the 118 flagged flow comparisons, two
Balerma ranked-subset consumer-chlorine differences above 0.01 mg/L, and one
0.000286 m TT ranked-subset EPANET pressure excess. These flags must be
reviewed; they are not claims of exact equivalence. A new or revised PDA
failure replay needs a separate matched failed-state check.
